Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Document Ref: EMB-PRV-2026

1. Data We Collect

At Ember, we value transparency and privacy. When you purchase products like the Youtube Creator Vault or interact with our web applications, we only gather basic information required to process and guarantee your access:

  • Transactional Contact: Your primary email address to send your instant download links and invoices.
  • Device & Telemetry: Anonymized browser type, screen resolution, and theme preference (`light` or `dark`) stored locally in your browser storage.
  • Payment Security: We do NOT collect or store your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs. All payments are encrypted by certified payment processors.

2. How Your Information is Used

Your data is strictly utilized for:

  • Delivering instant downloadable files and license certificates.
  • Providing product updates and updated resource files directly to your inbox.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing companies.
Your Rights: You can request complete deletion of your customer logs or opt out of release notifications anytime by emailing our support desk.

Return & Refund Policy

Digital Products & Immediate Access Terms

1. Nature of Instant Digital Downloads

Due to the immediate digital delivery of items such as the Youtube Creator Vault (which includes raw editing assets, templates, video plans, and direct cloud links), purchases are generally non-tangible and irrevocable once links are generated.

2. Qualifying Refund Conditions

We believe in 100% fair satisfaction. You are entitled to a full refund or replacement under the following scenarios:

  • Accidental Duplicate Orders: If your card or UPI was charged multiple times for the identical item, we will issue a direct refund for the redundant transaction within 24-48 hours.
  • Corrupt or Inaccessible Files: If the provided files cannot be downloaded or opened and our technical team cannot resolve it within 48 hours.
How to request: Send your payment receipt or Order ID along with your registered email to support@ember-studio.com or submit the quick contact ticket below.
